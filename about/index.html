The story behind the training.

VA Newbies International was built for people who were told they needed experience they did not have.

Founder and VA TrainerDiPhoenix, Arizona
  • Experience9+ years in digital work
  • Trained350+ virtual assistants
  • Served100+ clients worldwide
  • BasePhoenix, Arizona, USA

Coach Di never worked in a corporate job. Every role she looked at asked for experience she did not have. She started anyway.

She built her career as a virtual assistant and worked her way up. By her second year she was managing four clients at once. Over time she worked with authors, publishers and growing businesses in many countries.

She holds a bachelor's degree in Marketing Management from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines. She also holds a two year associate degree in Business Management from AMA Computer University.

Today she trains new virtual assistants. She has trained more than 350 of them. The training is simple. You learn a skill, you practice it with a real business, and you keep going until it feels natural.

VA Newbies International is open to English speakers from any country. Members start as newbies. They graduate as VA Newbies International members.
